# 📊 Statistical Analysis

> **A statistical framework for evaluating whether recurrent human TP53 cancer-associated residues exhibit distinctive evolutionary conservation across mammals.**

---

# 🎯 1. Statistical Objective

The primary statistical question is:

> **Do recurrent human TP53 cancer-associated hotspots exhibit different evolutionary conservation from an appropriate control set?**

The statistical analysis is performed after sequence curation, alignment, residue mapping, and conservation calculation.

---

# 🧬 2. Primary Hypotheses

### Null hypothesis — H₀

The conservation values of hotspot residues are not systematically different from those of the selected control residues.

### Alternative hypothesis — H₁

The conservation values of hotspot residues differ systematically from those of the selected control residues.

The statistical result is interpreted together with effect magnitude, uncertainty, and biological context.
