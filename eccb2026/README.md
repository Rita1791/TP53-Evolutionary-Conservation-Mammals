## 🧬 Research Presented at ECCB 2026

This repository section contains the materials associated with my accepted **poster presentation at ECCB 2026**, the European Conference on Computational Biology.

The presented work investigates the evolutionary conservation of recurrent human **TP53** cancer-associated mutation hotspots across mammalian species.

The central research question is:

> **Are recurrent human TP53 cancer-associated mutation hotspots located at residues that are evolutionarily constrained across mammals?**

The study integrates:

- 🧬 Comparative genomics
- 🧪 Cancer biology
- 🌳 Evolutionary biology
- 💻 Bioinformatics
- 📊 Sequence conservation analysis
- 🎯 Cancer mutation hotspot mapping
- 🧩 Structural interpretation
- 📈 Statistical analysis
- ♻️ Reproducible computational research
